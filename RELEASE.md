Nine original procedural pony CAD sculptures, created with Codex and collaborating LLM agents: the Mane Six, Princess Celestia, Princess Luna, and Clockwork Relativity, the user's unicorn OC and Luna's son.

![The full collection](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NoahWLono/equestria-atelier-cad/main/outputs/renders/collection_catalog.jpg)

Choose a download:

- **Complete collection · 321.1 MiB:** all nine STEP assemblies, unioned STL meshes, colored GLB scenes, the editable Blender studio, full-resolution portraits and orthographic views, Python source, build launcher, documentation, and verification reports.
- **STEP CAD · 5.2 MiB:** nine named, colored solid assemblies with dimensions and reports.
- **STL print meshes · 125.7 MiB:** nine joined ASCII STL meshes, printing guidance, and reports. Select millimeters in your slicer.

[Read the complete process](https://github.com/NoahWLono/equestria-atelier-cad/blob/main/PROCESS.md), including the modeling decisions, failed checks, precision fixes, rendering work, and final validation.

All nine saved STL files passed watertightness, consistent winding, positive volume, and one-shell checks. All STEP assemblies were imported back and checked. The collection contains 835 named CAD components and 2,455,018 STL triangles. Ten geometry acceptance tests passed. The images were rendered from the exported CAD geometry.

These are decorative fan-art sculptures. No physical test print is claimed. Native CAD application behavior and rebuilding outside Linux have not been tested. STEP components overlap intentionally; they are sculpting assemblies without mechanical joints or fit clearances.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` and `archives.json` provide exact archive hashes, sizes, and file counts. The complete ZIP includes its own file-level checksum manifest. The full process write-up is also attached separately as `PROCESS.md`.
